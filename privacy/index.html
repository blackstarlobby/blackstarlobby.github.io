Privacy Policy

Lobby by BlackStar · last updated 24 November 2026

Who we are

Lobby by BlackStar sells game-save services through its Discord server. This page covers this website (blackstarlobby.github.io) and our Discord bots "Backup" and "BlackStar Stats".

Our Discord bots

  • Backup reads only the product catalogue posts ("Shop") and per-game proof posts ("Proof") that our supplier's staff publish in two forum channels of the supplier's server, with the supplier's agreement, and reposts them in our own server. It does not read or process other members' messages, has no commands and sends nothing on the supplier's server.
  • BlackStar Stats reads, on our own server only, the arrivals channel (which invite link a member used) and our paid-ticket channels, to count how many customers each invite link brings.

What we store

On our own computer only: message and channel IDs, post titles (game names), a SHA-256 hash of each catalogue post to detect edits, and aggregated counts (arrivals and sales per invite link). We do not store message text from members, and we do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Artificial intelligence

No Discord message content is used to train machine-learning or AI models.

This website

This website uses the Meta Pixel to measure our ads: it records page views and clicks on the "Join on Discord" button. Meta processes this data under its own privacy policy. We do not collect names, e-mail addresses or payment details on this website.

Your choices and contact

To ask a question or have data about you removed, open a ticket on our Discord server: discord.gg/65ZuSu3PhC. Removed data is deleted within 30 days.
